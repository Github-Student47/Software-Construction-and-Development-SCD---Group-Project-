## Research Strategy
- Prioritize academic and industry authoritative sources
- Include recent developments (2020-2025) and established foundational materials
- Seek multiple perspectives from different stakeholders (developers, architects, researchers)
- Verify information across multiple credible sources
- Focus on practical applications and real-world implementations

## Target Sources
- Academic publications and conference papers
- Industry standards documentation (OMG, ISO, IEEE)
- Technical vendor documentation and whitepapers
- Expert blogs and thought leadership content
- Case studies and implementation reports
